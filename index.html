Payments pipeline

Authorize → authenticate → capture → ledger → settle
1AuthorizeValidate payment credentials and request issuer authorization.
2AuthenticateApply 3DS/SCA or wallet cryptogram validation when required.
3CaptureConfirm the authorized amount for clearing.
4LedgerPost immutable double-entry balance movements.
5SettleReconcile network settlement and merchant payout state.
Bank connection is unsupported.EZPay can support bank-payment rails such as ACH, but persistent consumer bank aggregation / online-banking credential connections are intentionally not part of this product.

01

Tokenization boundary

Raw PAN/CVV should never touch the EZPay dashboard or public web origin. Production collection belongs in a hardened PCI-scoped component or certified vaulting/tokenization environment.

02

Encryption

TLS in transit, envelope encryption at rest, KMS/HSM-backed key management, strict key rotation and separate environments.

03

Authentication

Passkeys/MFA for operators, scoped API keys, signed sessions, least privilege, device/session revocation, and immutable audit trails.

04

PCI & compliance

A real direct card processor must design around PCI DSS, network/acquirer obligations, dispute rules, monitoring, incident response, and applicable payments regulation.

05

Fraud controls

Velocity rules, fingerprinting, behavioral signals, allow/deny lists, 3DS/SCA, manual review and risk-score policy engines.

06

Ledger integrity

Append-only double-entry accounting, idempotency, event versioning, reconciliation, invariant checks and durable backup strategy.
